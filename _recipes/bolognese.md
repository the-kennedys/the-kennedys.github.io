Serves 3
Prep: 5 minutes
Cook: 1 hour

A simple bolognese, with a trivial switch from vegy mince to green lentils.

I've been trying to wean myself off canned green lentils onto dried ones in this recipe, and it's going pretty well. I need to trial adding wine.

## Ingredients ##

* 1 large onion, finely chooped
* 1 Carrot, finely chopped
* 1 Celery Stick, finely chopped
* 1 red pepper, finely chopped
* 1 clove garlic
* 1 Tin Chopped Tomatoes
* 1 tbs Tom Puree
* 1 tsp Basil
* 1 tsp Oregano
* 1 tsp Thyme
* 1 bay leaf
* 125g Green lentils
* 250ml Vegetable Stock
* 1 tsp marmite


## Method ##

- Fry the onion, carrot, celery and pepper for 10 minutes on a medium to low heat
- Add the garlic, fry for one minute
- Add the tomatoes, herbs and tomato puree, and stir well
- Add the lentils and stock. Bring to the boil, then simmer low and slow for 40 minutes

A fragrant, lightly spiced Moroccan vegetable stew loaded with chickpeas, carrot, and courgette in a warming cumin and coriander broth. Fresh coriander and mild red chilli give it a vibrant finish without needing any added salt.
Serves 2–3
Prep 10 min
Cook 25 min

# Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, roughly chopped
- 1 medium carrot, sliced into 1cm rounds
- 1 medium courgette, quartered lengthwise and sliced
- 1 mild red chilli, finely sliced (deseeded for less heat)
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 x 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 500ml low-sodium vegetable stock
- Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- Small handful fresh coriander, roughly chopped
- Lemon wedges, to serve

# Method

- Heat the olive oil in a large saucepan or casserole over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and sliced carrot, and cook for 6–8 minutes until the onion is soft and starting to turn golden.
- Stir in the garlic and sliced chilli, and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
- Add the ground cumin and ground coriander. Stir continuously for 1 minute to gently toast the spices in the oil.
- Pour in the 500ml vegetable stock, scraping up any toasted spices from the bottom of the pan.
- Add the rinsed chickpeas and bring the stew to a boil. Reduce heat to low, cover with a lid, and simmer gently for 12–15 minutes so the carrot begins to soften.
- Add the sliced courgettes, replace the lid, and simmer for another 8–10 minutes until the courgettes are tender but retain their bite.
- Remove from the heat. Season with freshly ground black pepper and squeeze in the juice from half a lemon.
- Scatter with plenty of freshly chopped coriander and serve hot with couscous, quinoa, or warm flatbread.

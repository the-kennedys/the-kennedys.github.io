A fantastic medium/hot curry using base gravy

From Al's Kitchen youtube channel.
Serves 2

# Ingredients

- 200g vegan chicken-style pieces (e.g. THIS Isn't Chicken, Vegetarian Butcher What The Cluck, or firm tofu — note: standard Quorn fillets contain egg white)
- 1 clove garlic 
- half tsp coriander 
- half tsp cumin 
- half tsp turmeric 
- ½ tap fenugreek leaf / kasoori methi 
- 1 tsp mild chilli powder  
- 1 tap mild curry powder  
- 1/3 tsp garam masala 
- 2 tbs tomato puree 
- ½ tsp salt but 
- Coriander 
- 1 tomato 

# Method 

- Fry garlic 
- Fry spices 
- Fry tomato puree 
- Fry off base gravy 
- Add salt 
- Add meat  
- Add tomatoes 
- Cook for 5 minutes

## Ingredients ##

* 1 tbsp Olive Oil
* 1 onion, finely chopped
* 1 head broccoli (approx. 200g)
* 1 vegetable stock cube
* 30g vegan blue cheese, crumbled (or 2 tbsp nutritional yeast + 1 tbsp tahini/white miso for a tangy, savoury kick)

## Method ##

- Fry the onion until soft
- Add the broccoli, vegetable stock cube and 500ml water
- Simmer for 10 minutes
- Blend with a stick blender
- Stir in the vegan cheese (or nutritional yeast and tahini/miso) until melted and creamy

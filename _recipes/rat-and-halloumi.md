From Fast 800 Cookbook (veganised — swapped halloumi for smoked tofu or vegan halloumi)

# Ingredients
Serves 2
- 1 tbsp Olive Oil
- 1 Onion, cut into wedges
- 1 Pepper, cut into 2 cm chunks
- 1 Courgette, quartered lengthways then sliced thinly
- 1 Clove Garlic, crushed
- 10g Fresh basil
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes
- 125g firm smoked tofu (or vegan halloumi), sliced into 4–6 pieces

# Method

- Bake the veg for 25 mins
- Stir and bake for another 10 mins
- Add the garlic, basil and tomatoes. Stir in. Top with the smoked tofu (or vegan halloumi), drizzled with a little oil
- Cook for 15 minutes

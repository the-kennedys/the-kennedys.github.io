# Ingredients 

- 1 small onion sliced  
- half green pepper sliced  
- 2 cloves garlic finely sliced  
- small piece of ginger finely sliced  
- 1.5 tsp mild madras curry powder  
- 1.5 tsp coriander powder  
- 0.5 tsp turmeric  
- 2 tsp cumin seeds  
- 3 cardamon pods (seeds) 
- 4 green chillies sliced  
- 1 mild red chilli  
- 2 tbsp tomato puree  
- 300ml base gravy 
- 1/2 tsp salt  
- “meat”  
- handful fresh coriander  
- half tomato sliced 

# Method 

- Fry onions and pepper until soft 
- Garlic and Ginger 
- Spices fry off 
- Green chillies and red chillies 
- Tomato puree 
- Splash of water, cook down 
- Base gravy (2 ladles) 
- Salt 
- Meat 
- Coriander 
- Tomatoes

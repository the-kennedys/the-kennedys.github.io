This base gravy is a weekend job, but it makes 5 portions each of which can make 2 curries

Transcribed from Al's Kitchen Youtube Channel, this is base gravy version 1. Use it with the other curry recipes, especially the [5 spice madras](/recipes/five-spice-madras). It freezes as well, so this is a month's worth of curry goodness.

Makes: 5 portions
prep: 10 minutes
cooking: 2 hours

# Ingredients

- 1kg Onions, peeled and halved
- 1 Red Pepper, sliced
- 1 Chopped Green Finger Chilli
- 1 Carrot, peeled and sliced
- 50g Peeled Garlic
- 50g Fresh Ginger
- 1tbsp Tomato Puree
- 4 tbsp oil
- 1tsp salt
- 30g Coriander
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes
- 1 tsp Coriander
- 1 tsp Cumin
- 1 tsp Turmeric
- 1 tsp Paprica

# Method

- Peel and half the onions
- Peel and slice the carrots
- Slice the peppers
- Finely slice the chillies
- Blend the ginger & garlic (might have to add a little water and veg oil)  
- Add 600ml water, the chopped red & green chilli peppers, salt, coriander leaf, veg oil, ginger & garlic paste to the pan and put the lid on and turn on the heat  
- After 15 minutes, give it all a stir and reduce to a simmer  
- After another 45 minutes, stir and turn the heat off and leave it to cool  
- Blend all of it with a hand blender or use a food processor until smooth and return to the pan  
- Blend the tinned tomatoes until smooth and place in a separate pan and simmer for 10 minutes (no lid) and add a little veg oil  
- Add the tomato puree, paprika, coriander powder, cumin powder and turmeric to the tomatoes  
- Simmer down for another 10 minutes (no lid)  
- Add the tomatoes to the large pan

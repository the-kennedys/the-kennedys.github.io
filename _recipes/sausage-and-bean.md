## Ingredients ##

* 1 large onion, roughly chopped
* 1 pack vegan sausages (e.g. Richmond Meat-Free or Beyond), sliced into 1cm slices
* 1 Tin Kidney Beans in chilli sauce
* 2 tsp Wholegrain Mustard
* 1 tsp Vegetable Bouillon

## Method ##

- Fry the onion and sausages, turning the slices to ensure they are browned
- Add the kidney beans, 300ml water, mustard and bouillon 
- Simmer for 5 minutes

## Alternative ##
- Put the mix into a casserole dish, add thinly sliced potato on top
- Baste the potato with melted vegan butter (or olive oil) and mustard
- Cook covered for 30 minutes, until the potato is cooked
